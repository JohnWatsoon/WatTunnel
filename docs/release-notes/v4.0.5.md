**Carrier re-tests and carrier switches work again, updates find a mirror when GitHub is blocked, and the panel gets Auto tune.**

Update both ends. On Kharej, the update repairs a broken auto tunnel within a few minutes (the next auto-heal run), with no new tunnel needed.

## Changes in detail

- **Re-test and Change carrier fixed.** Since 4.0.2, Kharej's auto-heal (the bash manager) wrongly put ptcp and spray into the main unit of an auto tunnel. The engine refuses that and stopped: the main unit restarted every 2 seconds (20,000+ times on our test server), and every direct carrier answered "no link in 8 s". So only the first test after creating a tunnel worked, and switching carriers always ended with "Not connected yet; restoring the previous config." Heal now keeps ptcp and spray in their own helper units and repairs a broken main unit on its next run.
- **Update mirrors.** When github.com cannot be reached and no mirror or proxy is set, `update` (the panel's Update too) tries ghfast.top, gh-proxy.com, ghproxy.net and github.moeyy.xyz in turn. The pinned-key signature still checks every file, so a mirror cannot change what gets installed.
- **Auto tune.** Every Iran tunnel's drawer has *Auto tune*: it tests every carrier, takes the fastest, tunes it and switches to it.
- **Actions look like Settings.** The drawer starts with a quick bar (Restart, Stop, Speed test, Diagnose), then rows like the settings: icon, name, one line on what it does. Three groups (Carrier, Ports and traffic, Pairing and removal) replace seven boxes of buttons.
- **Diagnose shows plain text.** Job logs no longer show terminal codes such as `[2A` or progress-bar boxes, and a speed bar shows its last value once instead of three times.
