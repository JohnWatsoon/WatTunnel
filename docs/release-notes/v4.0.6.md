**Switching an Iran tunnel to another reverse carrier works now.**

Update the Iran side. Kharej needs nothing new.

## Changes in detail

- **Reverse carrier switch fixed.** Picking a reverse carrier (tcp, tls, ws, wss, h2, h2c or grpc reverse) after a test, from *Change carrier*, *Re-test carriers* or *Auto tune*, wrote `listen: "17344"` instead of `listen: ":17344"`. The engine refused that address and stopped at once, so every such switch ended with "Not connected yet; restoring the previous config." The tunnel always went back to its old carrier, so nothing broke, but it never moved.
- **The switch waits for the link.** After the restart the manager now waits up to 25 s (45 s for reverse) for the new link, as the terminal menu always did, instead of checking once right away.
